Board members & auditor

Meet our board and auditor

Yoshihisa Honda本田 宜久

Representative Director

Director and Head of General Practice, Kaita Hospital (頴田病院)

A graduate of Nagasaki University School of Medicine, he worked in general practice and respiratory medicine at Aso Iizuka Hospital (飯塚病院) before becoming Director of Kaita Hospital (頴田病院) in 2008. He is a board-certified general internal medicine specialist and a designated supervisor for specialist training in general practice.

Yasuhiro Osugi大杉 泰弘

Board member

Professor of General and Family Medicine, Fujita Health University School of Medicine

A graduate of 藤田保健衛生大学 (now Fujita Health University), he practiced general medicine at Aso Iizuka Hospital (飯塚病院) and Kaita Hospital (頴田病院) and worked at Toyota Regional Medical Center (豊田地域医療センター). He became a professor at Fujita Health University in 2025 and leads its general practice training program.

Shin Yoshida吉田 伸

Board member

Deputy Director, Kaita Hospital (頴田病院)

A graduate of Nagoya City University Medical School, he completed initial residency at Aso Iizuka Hospital (飯塚病院), family medicine training through the Iizuka–Kaita program, and home medical care training at Kaita Hospital (頴田病院). A specialist in family medicine, general practice and home medical care, he leads the Iizuka–Kaita family medicine residency program.

Kazuki Matsushima松島 和樹

Board member

Director, 川崎在宅クリニック

A graduate of Kobe University School of Medicine, he completed initial residency at Kobe City Medical Center General Hospital and advanced training in the Iizuka–Kaita family medicine program. He is a specialist and supervisor in family medicine and home medical care, and has directed 川崎在宅クリニック since 2025.

Hiroki Miyachi宮地 紘樹

Board member

Director, Kakegawa East Hospital (掛川東病院)

A graduate of Nagoya University School of Medicine with a doctorate in medicine, he worked in cardiovascular and vascular surgery and home medical care before becoming Director of Kakegawa East Hospital (掛川東病院) in 2019. His specialties are general internal medicine and vascular surgery.

重冨 雄哉

Board member

Chair, medical corporations 光輝会 and 香流会

After serving as Deputy Director of 六日市病院 in Shimane, he became Chair of the medical corporations 光輝会 and 香流会. He oversees the operation of Kouki Hospital (光輝病院) in Yamaguchi.

Masashi Fujii藤井 将志

Board member

Director of Administration, Yatsuda Hospital (谷田病院)

A graduate of Waseda University’s School of Political Science and Economics, he worked in medical management consulting and as a management adviser at 沖縄県立中部病院. He became Director of Administration at Yatsuda Hospital (谷田病院) in 2015 and founded 医療環境総研 in 2020.

山内 剛志

Auditor

Head of Information Systems and Medical Administration Section Chief, 済生会みすみ病院

He obtained the Level 1 health care management qualification (医療経営士) in 2024. He has taught at training sessions jointly organized by the Japan Hospital Association and 病院経営管理士会, and lectures on health information systems and security.
